## Visual style
- Edge colors: AWS `#ED7100`, GCP `#4285F4`, Azure `#0078D4`.
- Solid = sync/request path; dashed = async, events, replication, protects, error/DLQ.
- Security, monitoring, and management services are cross-cutting — place them in dedicated group containers BELOW the main data flow. Do NOT connect data-flow edges to or from these cross-cutting services.

- Minimum 150px vertical gap between the last AZ container and the supporting groups below
- Each AZ container auto-sizes to hold its three tier subnets
